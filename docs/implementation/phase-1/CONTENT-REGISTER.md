## Evidence standards

For each retained claim, record the source, date and reviewer. Separate Instagram audience size from paying customers or product users. Do not reuse social-media engagement as proof of product adoption.

Confirm native-store availability through a current check rather than the presence of an App Store link in source. Verify the support mailbox through owner confirmation or an authorized delivery check. Do not send test emails without permission.

Record testimonial provenance before reuse. Do not invent replacement quotes, ratings, credentials or outcome guarantees.

Privacy and commercial terms require factual product decisions. Phase 1 should identify missing facts rather than publish invented legal promises. Handle substantive policy drafting in a separate reviewed task.

## Editorial review checklist

- [ ] Brand spelling and domain are consistent.
- [ ] Public copy distinguishes today's availability from future plans.
- [ ] No recipe or checkout CTA leads to an absent destination.
- [ ] AI, milestone and future parenting tools are excluded from the first purchase promise.
- [ ] Native-user support remains accurate where required.
- [ ] Support channel and service expectations match reality.
- [ ] Claims in page titles, descriptions, cards and image text receive the same review.
- [ ] Dormant components are marked for review before later reuse.
- [ ] Open commercial decisions remain visibly unresolved in planning documents.

Do not check these boxes until the implementation evidence exists.
