# Content and launch communications

## Launch content package

Prepare one approved source of truth for collection title, exact member count, one-time price/currency, inclusions, free samples, print access, refund terms, future additions and hosted access wording. Reuse this record across landing/collection page, Checkout, receipts, support and Tiny Soho posts.

Use the approved brand relationship: “Recipes by Tiny Soho, inside My Curated Haven.” Lead with simple toddler recipes for busy families. Do not promote unfinished milestones, unlimited AI, an app-store launch or a subscription.

Verify current reviewed revisions and actual deployed slot mapping before publication. Synthetic `synth-free-*` fixtures use different IDs and are not substitutes for the production manifest.

For every free and paid recipe, record ingredients/units, method, yield/time, reviewed allergen/diet tags, storage wording, image rights, source revision and editor. Empty allergen metadata does not justify an allergen-free claim. Resolve uncertain food-safety wording through qualified editorial review rather than adding advice in a deployment script.

Publish through a targeted reviewed change with expected row counts and manifest hash. Check anonymous free read, nonbuyer paid denial, owner paid read and storage policy afterward. Never bulk rerun ingestion over reviewed live content as a shortcut.

## Updates after purchase

Keep purchased membership and history immutable according to Phase 8. Distinguish a correction to an existing recipe from a new recipe or new collection. Record revision, reason, editor, affected releases, cache invalidation and customer impact.

If content is unsafe or materially inaccurate, restrict the affected recipe promptly under the approved policy, preserve the order/history and provide a support path. Editorial and commerce decide the corrected content or customer remedy. Do not quietly remove a purchased member or overwrite the manifest.

Future additions are included only if the approved commercial policy says so. The first launch must not make an accidental lifetime/all-future-content promise through a caption or receipt.

## Tiny Soho funnel

| Placement | Entry page | Intended next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile link | Official recipe-first landing or `/recipes` | Read a complete free recipe |
| Recipe-specific post/story | Matching free recipe page | Cook/read/print, then explore the collection |
| Invited launch message | Approved collection page with account eligibility explanation | Sign in with invited identity and review offer |
| Wider collection announcement | Approved collection page | Review exact contents and one-time purchase |

Use a small registered campaign-code set from Phase 9. Do not put email, child details, user IDs or sensitive interests into URLs. Campaign tags are attribution labels, never access credentials.

Test final links in actual Instagram iOS/Android browsers. Check the landing state, auth/Checkout handoff, return recovery, canonical domain and copy consistency. A public social link might be shared beyond the intended cohort, so server eligibility still applies before L4.

## Communication approval record

For each external placement, record channel, account, exact copy/asset version, URL/campaign, intended recipients/audience, sender, scheduled time, stage prerequisites and pause owner. Obtain the applicable approval before sending or scheduling. Writing this plan sends nothing.

Avoid fabricated scarcity, unapproved discounts, unsupported nutrition/safety claims and promises not present in the offer. A limited invited launch describes availability honestly without suggesting the digital recipe supply will run out.

Prepare useful fallback copy for checkout unavailable, processing delayed and recipe under review. Explain the next action and support route. Do not expose database/registry/internal exception text to parents.

## Preserved future pages and search

Keep the deferred source work from Phase 1. Verify unfinished `/features`, `/resources`, `/careers` and duplicate contact content stay outside the approved public navigation/discovery behaviour. Hidden navigation alone is not privacy.

Confirm public production pages are indexable as intended, protected staging stays protected/nonindexable, and account/callback/owned content is excluded. Free structured data should reflect visible reviewed content. Paid recipe bodies stay out of public HTML, RSC, JSON-LD, shared cache and social previews.

## Corrections and launch pauses

The marketing owner must know how to pause scheduled placements. Removing a post does not stop traffic already following shared links. Coordinate communication pause with engineering's server-side stop when payment/service integrity is affected.

For a material recipe or access issue, record affected audience and approved message before notification. Avoid broad customer disclosure when a targeted notice suffices. Retain a sanitised correction history for the release review.
