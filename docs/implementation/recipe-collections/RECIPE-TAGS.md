# Recipe tags and collection placement (proposal)

Status: proposed 2026-10-05 for owner review, before the admin panel exists. The admin panel can import [recipe-tags.json](recipe-tags.json); this page is the readable copy. Vocabulary and rules: [COLLECTIONS.md](COLLECTIONS.md).

**In short:** all 70 catalog recipes are tagged. **14 of 20 collections** already have 8–12 recipes and can be created now. **6 are short** and need new recipes: First Finger Foods (7), First Breakfasts (2), Fruit Gummies (0), Big-Kid Breakfasts (4), Thanksgiving Table (7), Holiday Baking (4). The catalog is a year old and leans to purées and toddler meals, so the gaps are breakfasts, gummies and baking, which is what the last three months of Instagram recipes cover.

Example: *Veggie Lentil Pancakes* (from 12 months) is tagged `1–2 y, 2–3 y · breakfast, lunch · protein, iron, fiber, veg-packed · freezes, make-ahead · egg-free, dairy-free, nut-free, gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan · finger food`, and placed in Toddler Breakfasts and Meal Prep for Toddlers.

## How the tags were set

- **Stage** comes from each recipe's reviewed age range. Bands run 6–8 m, 9–12 m, 1–2 y, 2–3 y, 3–5 y; a recipe from 12 months starts at 1–2 y.
- **Free-from** comes only from the reviewed allergen list (egg-free = no egg allergen; nut-free = no tree nuts or peanuts). Gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan also need the recipe's reviewed diet tag.
- **Freezes** and **make-ahead** come from each recipe's storage note; **under 15 min** from its total time.
- **Goal, other practical notes, texture and collection placement** are judgement calls, listed per recipe below to change freely.
- The three live collections (Halloween, Meal Prep for Toddlers, Protein Packs) keep exactly the recipes they show today.
- The three free sample recipes are tagged but not placed in paid collections, so nobody pays for a recipe that is free.

## Things to decide

1. **Ages for 10 recipes were never set in the catalog.** I inferred a starting age for each; please confirm:
   - Baked Sweet Potato with Black Beans and Avocado: from 12 months. soft mashed beans and sweet potato suit from about 12 months.
   - Chicken and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Brown Rice: from 12 months. stir-fry with soy and sesame, cut small, from 12 months.
   - Folate-Rich Lentil and Spinach Soup: from 9 months. blended lentil soup suits from about 9 months.
   - Greek Yogurt Parfait with Berries and Almonds: from 12 months. contains honey (not before 12 months). Leave out or finely grind the almonds for under-4s.
   - Lentil and Vegetable Curry with Coconut Milk: from 9 months. soft lentil curry, mashed, from about 9 months.
   - Mediterranean Chicken and Chickpea Salad: from 18 months. quarter chickpeas and olives; from about 18 months.
   - Overnight Oats with Chia and Berries: from 12 months. contains honey or maple (honey not before 12 months).
   - Quinoa and Black Bean Burrito Bowl: from 12 months. from about 12 months.
   - Salmon and Sweet Potato Bowl with Avocado: from 12 months. flaked salmon and mashed sweet potato, from about 12 months.
   - Spinach and Feta Egg Muffins: from 12 months. egg muffins cut into fingers, from about 12 months.
2. **Overlap between books.** 70 recipes fill about 152 places, so many recipes sit in several books. Most shared:
   - Savory Corn & Cheddar Muffins: 5 collections
   - Cinnamon Sweet Potato Snack Fries with Yogurt Dip: 5 collections
   - Soft Banana Oat Toddler Cookies: 5 collections
   - Avocado & Black Bean Quesadilla Strips: 4 collections
   - Mild Curried Red Lentil Cakes: 4 collections
   - Mini Turkey & Apple Meatloaf Muffins: 4 collections
   A parent who buys First Tastes and Batch & Freeze for Babies gets 6 of the same purées twice. New recipes should go first into the books with the most overlap; later the site can show "you already own 3 of these".
3. **Recipes in no collection:** Rainbow Pesto Pasta Salad, Cold Sesame Noodle Salad, Soft Tofu Veggie Stir Fry with Rice. The two noodle and pasta salads contain peanuts or tree nuts, so they stay out of Lunchbox & Daycare (many daycares are nut-free).
4. **Recipes that start later than their book's stage** carry a card note ("from 18 months"); see the dashes in the lists above.
