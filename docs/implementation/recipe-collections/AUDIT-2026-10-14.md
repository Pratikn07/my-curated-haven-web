# Recipe and collection audit, 2026-10-14

Owner request of 2026-10-14: go through every recipe in every collection, fix what is wrong, then make three recipes free in each collection, choosing the best. This page records what was checked, what changed (in this repository and in production) and what is left for the owner.

**In short:** the 90 recipes and 20 collections were checked against each other, against the rules in [COLLECTIONS.md](COLLECTIONS.md) and against production. Two allergen lists in [recipe-tags.json](recipe-tags.json) missed wheat, the free-sample flags were stale, Fruit Gummies was empty although the catalog has two real-fruit gummies, and the rule checker could not read the Halloween recipes' allergen names. All fixed. Production now has 28 free recipes: the owner's three Halloween ones and three per collection wherever the overlaps allow it (17 of 20 collections at exactly three), which meant publishing 25 drafts.

## Free samples

### How they were chosen

1. Each collection should show three free recipes, the best proof of the book: strong on its theme, short ingredient lists, nothing a parent has to guess at, and together covering the book's range (a vegetable, a grain and a fruit purée; a bite, a strip and a flake; one protein each).
2. A recipe is free everywhere or nowhere, and 46 recipes sit in two or more books, so freeing one for a book counts in every book it is in. The set was solved so that every open book (the eleven with a page) has exactly three, then the coming-soon books were filled with as few extra recipes as possible.
3. Halloween keeps the owner's three of 2026-10-13, so the Halloween menu still reads "2 free · 1 in the book".
4. Owner-approved rewrites (#28, #30, #31, #34, #36, #37) were preferred where they fit. Recipes that sit in four or five books (the slow-cooker stew, the one-pot chicken rice, the burritos, the meatloaf muffins) stay paid, so each book keeps its reasons to buy.

The three exceptions: Holiday Baking has one recipe, so that one is free; Birthday Party reaches two (its third would be Samosa Puffs or Pizza Scrolls, and either would push Lunchbox & Daycare or Picky Eater Favorites to four); Fruit Gummies has none, because both its recipes are in the Halloween book.

## Production change

Run on 2026-10-14 through the Supabase connection, as one atomic block (a failure anywhere rolls the whole thing back):

1. `UPDATE public.recipe_catalog SET publication_state = 'published', published_at = now()` for the 25 free recipes that were drafts (the trigger `recipe_catalog_publish_requires_review` checked each one; all 90 pass the review gate). The three Halloween free recipes were already published.
2. `private.publish_free_recipe_slots(...)` with the 28 ids in slot order: the Halloween three first (slots 1–3 as before), then the baby recipes, then the toddler and family ones.

Checked afterwards: 48 recipes published (20 Halloween, 3 launch, 25 new), 42 drafts, 28 free slots, every free recipe published. Nothing else was touched: no recipe text, no prices or offers, no release, no entitlement, no collection availability.

What a visitor sees: `/recipes` lists 48 recipes, the 28 free ones first in slot order and the rest locked to their book; each open collection page shows "3 free to cook now" on its rows and three cards under "Try Free Sample Recipes"; `/halloween` is unchanged. Collection pages and `/halloween` are cached, so they can show the old state for up to an hour after the change.

Rollback, back to the 2026-10-13 state (the Halloween three free, the 25 back to drafts), in this order because a free recipe must be published:

```sql
DO $$
DECLARE
  v_free uuid[];
  v_drafts text[] := ARRAY[
    'silky-carrot-apple-starter-pure','asian-pear-rice-congee-pure','banana-avocado-breakfast-pure-with-yogurt',
    'mild-mexican-black-bean-sweet-corn-pure','baked-sweet-potato-with-black-beans-and-avocado','folate-rich-lentil-and-spinach-soup',
    'cheesy-broccoli-and-quinoa-bites','pumpkin-and-ricotta-gnocchi-pillows','salmon-and-sweet-potato-bowl-with-avocado',
    'spinach-and-feta-egg-muffins','overnight-oats-with-chia-and-berries','apple-cinnamon-oat-toddler-porridge',
    'greek-yogurt-parfait-with-berries-and-almonds','mild-vegetable-curry-with-rice','veggie-packed-lasagna',
    'mini-baked-chicken-nuggets','salmon-potato-veggie-mash','soft-tofu-veggie-stir-fry-with-rice',
    'savory-corn-and-cheddar-muffins','greek-pita-bento-box','cinnamon-sweet-potato-snack-fries-with-yogurt-dip',
    'baked-fish-tacos-with-slaw','mini-berry-yogurt-pops','soft-banana-oat-toddler-cookies','soft-date-almond-cocoa-bites'];
BEGIN
  SELECT array_agg(c.id ORDER BY s.ord) INTO v_free
    FROM unnest(ARRAY['berry-ghost-yogurt-bark','spider-cracker-bites','apple-pie-halloween-pockets']) WITH ORDINALITY AS s(slug, ord)
    JOIN public.recipe_catalog c ON c.slug = s.slug;
  PERFORM private.publish_free_recipe_slots(v_free);
  UPDATE public.recipe_catalog
     SET publication_state = 'draft', published_at = NULL, updated_at = now()
   WHERE slug = ANY(v_drafts) AND publication_state = 'published';
END $$;
```

To change the selection instead, call `private.publish_free_recipe_slots` with the new ordered list (it replaces the whole list), publish any draft first, and update `free_sample` in `recipe-tags.json` so the checker agrees.
