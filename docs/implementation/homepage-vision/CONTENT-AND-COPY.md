# Content and copy register

All copy below is a proposed implementation draft. The owner reviews the rendered page and final asset captions before public release. Existing approved commercial terms remain authoritative.

## Brand hierarchy

My Curated Haven is the parent product. Tiny Soho is the recipe/content connection. Use the exact line “Recipes by Tiny Soho, inside My Curated Haven.” in the recipe section and relevant collection context. Avoid “My Curated Heaven,” “TinySoho” or presenting Tiny Soho as an unrelated storefront.

Introduce the broader purpose in the H1 and subcopy. Recipe-specific acquisition copy still belongs on `/recipes` and collection pages. The homepage need not carry every keyword or explain every future capability.

## Collection section contract

Render only approved fields: collection name, reviewed summary, exact included contents/count if approved, printable-page inclusion, one-time purchase wording and canonical collection link. Optional price/currency must come from the same approved offer contract used by the collection page. Never insert the synthetic $15 fixture.

Preferred CTA: “View the recipe collection.” Keep payment on the existing guarded purchase journey. The homepage does not create a checkout session. If price or membership is undecided, omit those fields and the sale section rather than substituting “from” pricing or an unapproved count.

Do not claim lifetime access, all future recipes, AI credits, milestone tracking or future parenting tools are included. Refund and future-addition terms link to the existing approved offer/terms. This effort invents none of those decisions.

## FAQ drafts

**What is available on the website now?** Use the active presentation state. Preparation: “We're preparing the recipe experience. The other product areas shown here are previews.” Free-ready: “You have access to three complete free recipes. The paid collection is still being prepared.” Collection-ready: “Explore three free recipes or view the separate paid recipe collection. Parenting Chat, Curated Shop and Bloom are previews.”

**Are Parenting Chat, Curated Shop and Bloom available?** “These sections are sneak peeks. The web features are planned for later releases. We have no release dates to share yet.”

**Does buying recipes include the future features?** “The purchase covers the recipe collection described on its product page. Future parenting features are separate.” Show this question only when a paid collection is described, or phrase conditionally during preparation.

**Do I need an account for free recipes?** When verified: “No. Read and print the free recipes without creating an account. Account features and purchased access follow their own sign-in steps.” Preparation copy describes this as the planned free-recipe experience.

## Claims requiring evidence

| Claim | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Available now | Confirm the live destination, data and access path |
| In development | Use only if implementation work is underway, not merely planned |
| Planned for the web | Approved direction, no date or contractual entitlement implied |
| Expert-vetted / clinician-approved | Omit without named review evidence and scope |
| Safe / tested / allergy-safe products or recipes | Omit unsupported blanket assurances |
| Personalised / remembers your family | Describe as a preview concept, not a currently available web capability |
| Parent-tested / thousands of parents / ratings | Use only substantiated figures with provenance and publication permission |
| Product price / stock / retailer logo | Omit from static concept assets unless rights and freshness are explicitly managed |
| Chat diagnosis or emergency assistance | Exclude from homepage examples and claims |
| Community or expert marketplace | Exclude, not established as the reviewed four-pillar product |

## Metadata draft

Proposed homepage title: “My Curated Haven | Recipes and a glimpse of what's ahead”. Proposed description: “Meet My Curated Haven, starting with toddler recipes by Tiny Soho. Preview the Parenting Chat, Curated Shop and Bloom experiences planned for the web.”

Adjust the first sentence for preparation state so search snippets do not claim working recipes before release. The social image should pair the brand with “Starting with recipes” and clearly label the remaining pillars as previews. Do not advertise a live AI app, download or marketplace in Open Graph text while the page describes future web features.

## Editing workflow

Maintain one typed content source and a versioned register. Review hero, labels, visuals, alt text, metadata and FAQ as one package. Avoid duplicated status strings with conflicting meanings. After approval, changes to availability or purchase promises require a fresh content/state check, not an isolated copy edit.
